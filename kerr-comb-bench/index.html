Model: normalized LLE with second and third-order dispersion, one shifted mode for an avoided crossing, and a first-order thermal shift. Not included: Raman, higher-order modes, back-scattering, photorefraction, χ⁽²⁾ effects. Threshold estimate Pth ≈ κ²/(8ηγL·FSR²) with group index 2.1; time and thermal scales are normalized for speed. Source on GitHub.
